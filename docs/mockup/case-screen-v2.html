    Stages and rules

    Stages

    1. Arrived. The complaint is logged with how and when it came in.
    2. Routed. The complaint goes to one of seven departments by complaint type.
    3. In review. A handler checks the AI draft summary against the source notes, claim by claim.
    4. Handed over. The handover pack goes to the specialist team.
    5. Accepted. The specialist team accepts the pack.

    Rules

    • Urgent fast path. A case that arrives with signs of financial hardship or vulnerability skips the routing wait and goes straight to review.
    • Return. A specialist team can return a pack to the handler once, with a reason.
    • Second return. A pack returned twice goes to the team lead, not back to the handler.
    • Approval. A handover stays locked until a person has confirmed every AI claim.
    • Deadline. Each complaint has 35 business days from arrival. The warning period is chosen in Settings.

    Database notes

    • The tool reads the bank's systems and never writes back to them, except the handover it sends.
    • Personal details are masked before any text reaches the model.
    • The audit log only accepts new rows.
    • SQLite suits one demo user. A real deployment would use the bank's own database with access by role.
    • Figures on the Overview show their refresh time so nobody acts on stale counts.
