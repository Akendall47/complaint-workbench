# The brief

Source: Ankura invitation to the practical build task for the AI by Design Summer Internship 2027, Digital AI Advisory (reference R105080). Contact details have been removed.

## The task

Choose one of four fictional client scenarios and create a working prototype that helps address the client's problem. The candidate decides what to build and which AI tools to use. Keep the scope manageable and use only fictional or synthetic data.

The submission should consider:

- The potential business value
- Relevant risks, controls and human oversight
- How people's roles or workflows may change
- Any assumptions, limitations or trade-offs
- The AI tools used and the candidate's assessment of them

These reflect Ankura's AI Strategy, Governance Engineering and Workforce AI Transformation solution sets.

## What to submit

1. A working build: a link or the files, with clear instructions so that it can be opened and tested.
2. A supporting slide deck or document of no more than five pages covering:
   - The client problem and why the approach was chosen
   - How the prototype was built and how AI tools were used
   - What was tested, including the results and limitations
   - The controls, human review requirements and potential effects on staff

Submission may be a link to a GitHub repository, the package itself or a hosted site. The build and the supporting explanation are reviewed together.

## Scenario chosen: Banking, complaint handling

A fictional bank has a growing backlog of customer complaints. Staff spend time finding information across case notes and policy documents, and specialist teams receive incomplete handovers.

The bank wants to decide where AI could improve the service. The proposed approach needs to protect customer information, keep decisions reviewable and consider how complaints staff and specialists would use the solution.

Task: build something that helps the bank address this problem. Explain the expected value, the controls and human review, and the changes to staff roles or handoffs. Choose the scope and make any assumptions explicit.
