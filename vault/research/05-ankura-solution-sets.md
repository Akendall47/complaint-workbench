# Ankura's AI by Design practice and the stated criteria

Research agent report, 2026-11-10. Only the AI by Design page was fetched in full; the rest comes from search snippets. Ankura's internal marking is not public, so the mapping at the end is inference. Related: [[01-complaint-rules]], [[06-interface-design]].

## The three solution sets

- AI by Design serves EMEA clients and is led from London. Its premise is that the bottleneck is organisational, not technical. https://ankura.com/solutions/ai-by-design
- AI Strategy: "Ankura helps boards and leadership teams identify where AI can create growth opportunities".
- AI Governance Engineering: "Ankura enables organizations to effectively manage AI risk and unlock value at scale". Framed as an engineering and user-experience problem, not only policy.
- Workforce Transformation: "We redesign roles, reskill people and embed human-centered change".

## Recurring themes in Ankura's writing

- Governance built in from the start accelerates value: "do not bolt it on like a brake". https://ankura.com/insights/upfront-ai-governance-accelerates-value-do-not-bolt-it-on-like-a-brake
- Getting AI from pilot to production in financial services. https://ankura.com/insights/from-pilot-to-production-a-practical-ai-deployment-playbook-for-financial-institutions
- Agents need documentation and audit. https://ankura.com/insights/from-models-to-agents-rethinking-intelligence-in-financial-systems
- Vocabulary: ambition-to-adoption gap, pilot to production, governance as enabler, human-centred change, audit trail.

## Workforce evidence

- Brynjolfsson, Li and Raymond (QJE 2025): about 14% more issues resolved per hour with AI assistance, 34% for novices, minimal effect on experts. https://www.nber.org/papers/w31161
- Crowston and Bolici (2025) treat this levelling as a form of deskilling. https://crowston.syr.edu/sites/default/files/GAI_and_skills.pdf
- No field study of complaint handlers specifically was found.

## Mapping the five considerations (inference)

| Consideration | Solution set | Evidence that would satisfy it |
|---|---|---|
| Business value | AI Strategy | Value levers, a baseline, a pilot measure, a cost range |
| Risks, controls, human oversight | Governance Engineering | Risk register, approval gates, audit log, vulnerable-customer checks, go-live criteria |
| Role and workflow change | Workforce Transformation | Before-and-after responsibilities, novice and expert impact, training plan |
| Assumptions, limits, trade-offs | Governance and Strategy | Explicit assumptions, data limits, named trade-offs |
| AI tools used and assessment | Tool-agnostic | Candid review of each tool: where it helped, where it failed, how output was checked |
