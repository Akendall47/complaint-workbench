# Departments view

For specialist teams and the team lead. It shows one team's workload, its incoming handovers, and the fields most often missing. It also filters by source channel and by complaint type. A specialist accepts a handover or returns it with a reason. Departments: Fraud claims, Financial difficulty, Bereavement, Data protection, Mis-selling review, General service. Sources: phone, mobile app, secure message, branch, letter, email.
