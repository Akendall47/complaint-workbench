# Overview dashboard

The landing view, for the team lead's morning check. Headline figures: open, due within 5 business days, past deadline, average days open, share of handovers returned as incomplete. Charts: 8-week trend of new versus closed complaints, open complaints by department, complaints by source, cases by days left to deadline. Clicking a chart segment opens the matching filtered view. It also holds the [[complaint-map]].
