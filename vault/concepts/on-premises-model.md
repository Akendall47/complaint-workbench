# On-premises model

An open-weight model runs on the bank's own hardware, so customer data never needs to leave it. Research note 03 lists models that fit 8 GB of VRAM and says Apache-2.0 and MIT are the licences a bank's legal team accepts most easily (much of that note comes from blogs). The prototype records its answers from an Apache-2.0 Qwen model running locally through Ollama. No bank or regulator quote was found on cost predictability or auditability.
