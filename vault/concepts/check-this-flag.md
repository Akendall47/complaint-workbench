# "Check this" flag

Claims the model inferred rather than read, such as possible vulnerability, carry a "check this" flag instead of a confidence percentage. Google PAIR says whether and how to show confidence is a design decision (snippet only, UNVERIFIED). Research note 07 states that there is no verified evidence on confidence percentages. This is our own design reasoning.
