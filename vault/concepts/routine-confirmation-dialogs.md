# Routine confirmation dialogs

NN/g: "if you warn people too much, they stop paying attention"; reflexive confirmation "provides no protection at all" (read in full). So the product has no "Are you sure?" on routine actions. Research note 07 recommends undo and logged reasons in place of warnings. A risk by analogy: confirming each claim could also become a reflex. That part is our reasoning, and it is stated as a limitation.
