# Human approval

The AI finds, summarises and drafts. A person approves everything. ICO draft guidance on automated decision-making (31 March 2026) says human involvement must be active, not tokenistic, with authority to override (UNVERIFIED: from a search summary). The design makes approval active: Approve stays locked until every claim is confirmed, edited or removed.
