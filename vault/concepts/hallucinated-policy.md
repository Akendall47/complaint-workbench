# Hallucinated policy

A model can invent a rule that does not exist. In Moffatt v Air Canada (2024) a chatbot invented a bereavement policy and the airline was held liable (UNVERIFIED: search summary). In the prototype, policy clauses come from search, never from the model. Each clause has a number and opens in the policy library.
