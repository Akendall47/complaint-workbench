# Handover pack

The pack lists the fields the receiving department needs. Missing fields are named, with the action needed to fill them. A pack sent with fields missing is marked incomplete. The model suggests field values; a person checks them. The field list per category in research note 01 is the research agent's own synthesis: an assumption of the prototype, not a regulatory list. Required fields per department live in the configuration file.
