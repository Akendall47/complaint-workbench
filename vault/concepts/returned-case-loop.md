# Returned-case loop

A specialist can return an incomplete handover with a reason. The case goes back to its handler's queue, at the top. A case returned a second time goes to the team lead. The share of handovers returned as incomplete is a headline figure on the dashboard. This answers the brief's problem of incomplete handovers. No research source: our reasoning.

**Where it comes from:** the design document and the brief. The handover fields it checks are in [[01-complaint-rules]].
