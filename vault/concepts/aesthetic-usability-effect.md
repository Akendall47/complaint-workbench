# Aesthetic-usability effect

NN/g's account of the effect (Kurosu and Kashimura 1995) warns that a positive response to looks "masked its usability issues and made them harder to identify". So a polished style is not evidence that the tool is usable. Usability is checked separately by the browser tests and the keyboard-only walk-through.
