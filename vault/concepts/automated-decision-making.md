# Automated decision-making

The Data (Use and Access) Act 2025 replaced Article 22 with Articles 22A to 22D, moving from a prohibition to a safeguards regime, in force 5 February 2026 (law-firm source; not marked as read in full). ICO draft guidance of 31 March 2026 says human involvement must be active, not tokenistic, with authority to override (UNVERIFIED: search summary). The prototype does not decide a complaint or recommend an outcome or redress. Not researched: special category data, data minimisation, ICO guidance on AI.
