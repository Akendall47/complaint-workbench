# Automation bias

People tend to accept a machine's suggestion without checking it. A 2025 review of 35 studies found that explanations and transparency often fail to reduce it (snippet only, UNVERIFIED). A 2026 paper found explanations raised trust and increased over-reliance (snippet only, UNVERIFIED). Bansal et al. found "explanations increased the chance that humans will accept the AI's recommendation, regardless of its correctness" (abstract only, UNVERIFIED). So a source shown beside each claim is not enough on its own.
