# Complaint deadlines

A complaint has clocks that run from the day it is received. UNVERIFIED (from memory; the FCA Handbook pages could not be fetched): summary resolution by close of business on the third business day; final response within 8 weeks; payment-services complaints 15 business days, extendable to 35. The prototype works out deadlines with plain rules read from configuration, because regulatory clocks must not be probabilistic. Each limit is checked against the primary text before it is quoted.
