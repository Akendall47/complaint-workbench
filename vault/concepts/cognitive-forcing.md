# Cognitive forcing

The interface makes the person think before accepting the machine's answer. Buçinca, Malaya and Gajos found "cognitive forcing significantly reduced overreliance compared to the simple explainable AI approaches" (abstract only, UNVERIFIED). Their experiment did not test per-claim confirmation, so our control applies the principle and is not a tested design. Known costs: people rated the designs that worked best least favourably, and the benefit was smaller for some people. Research note 06 calls the same idea deliberate friction.
