# Response-time limits

NN/g: 0.1 s feels instant, 1 s keeps thought uninterrupted, 10 s is the attention limit (read in full). Core Web Vitals "good": LCP at most 2.5 s, INP at most 200 ms, CLS at most 0.1 (read in full). The design targets server response under 100 ms at the 95th percentile, measured by a benchmark script. Not verified, so not claimed: the Doherty threshold's original paper.
