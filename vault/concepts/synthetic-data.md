# Synthetic data

The brief allows fictional or synthetic data only (the brief was read in full). A seeded generator produces at least 120 cases. No Kaggle or regulator complaint text is used, because public complaint datasets contain real people's complaints. Proportions are loosely shaped on published FCA totals and described as illustrative; research note 01 does not say those pages were read in full. Limitation: classifier accuracy and masking patterns are tuned to synthetic text.
