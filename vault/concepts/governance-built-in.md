# Governance built in

Ankura frames AI governance as an engineering and user-experience problem, not only policy (AI by Design page, fetched in full). The prototype is built to show that. Its controls are visible features: masking, source-linked claims, per-claim confirmation, a reason on rejection, an audit trail.
