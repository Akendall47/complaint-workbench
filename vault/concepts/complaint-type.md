# Complaint type

Each case carries a second label beside its department: what the complaint is about. Examples: website or app, service, fees, fraud or security, investment advice, account administration. The same kind of small classifier that routes to a department predicts it, and shows the words that drove the prediction. The owner asked for it. No research note covers it.
