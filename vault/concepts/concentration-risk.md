# Concentration risk

Many firms depend on the same few outside providers. The Bank of England and FCA 2026 AI survey reports third-party systems as 39% of all AI systems and 53% of high-materiality ones; the top three cloud providers hold 79% of reported relationships. An April 2026 PRA letter says firms using third-party AI models should assess concentration risk. HM Treasury designated AWS, Google Cloud, Microsoft and Oracle as critical third parties on 13 July 2026.
