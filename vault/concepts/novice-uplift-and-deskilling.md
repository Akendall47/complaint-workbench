# Novice uplift and deskilling

Brynjolfsson, Li and Raymond (QJE 2025): about 14% more issues resolved per hour with AI assistance, 34% for novices, minimal effect on experts. Crowston and Bolici (2025) treat this levelling as a form of deskilling. No field study of complaint handlers was found.
