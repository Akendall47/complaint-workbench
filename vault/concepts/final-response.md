# Final response

UNVERIFIED (from memory, DISP 1.6): a final response is due within 8 weeks. It must accept the complaint, offer redress, or reject it with reasons, and must explain Financial Ombudsman Service rights and the 6-month referral window. From 1 June 2026, acknowledgements must state that a written response will come within 8 weeks (source: a member briefing). The prototype does not draft replies to customers or recommend an outcome.
