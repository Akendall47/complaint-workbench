# Minimal style, effective wins

**Decision:** Minimal and elegant in the manner of Apple's own apps; where that conflicts with dense case work, effective wins.

**Who decided:** Owner. Styling fallback: my call.

**Status:** The look is not approved yet. The first mockup was rejected by the owner. Claude and Codex design together: one drafts, the other critiques ([[cross-model-review]]).

**Fixed rules:** system fonts, no external requests, status never by colour alone, WCAG 2.2 AA, fully keyboard operable, inline SVG charts with values labelled.

**Evidence:** [[colour-not-alone]], [[accessibility-baseline]], [[bars-not-pies]], [[glanceable-dashboard]] (read in full, [[07-ux-evidence]]).

**Trade-off:** [[aesthetic-usability-effect]]: good looks can hide problems (UNVERIFIED). [[heatmap-caveat]]: the evidence is against the heatmap.

**What would change it:** The owner's sign-off on a mockup.
