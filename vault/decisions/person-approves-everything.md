# A person approves everything

**Decision:** The AI finds, summarises and drafts; a person approves everything, and the AI never decides a complaint.

**Who decided:** Not tagged in the design document. Listed under "Decisions so far" in [[Home]].

**Why:** The brief asks for decisions that stay reviewable. The design's anti-goal: the submission fails if the AI appears to decide a complaint, or a case can be approved without a person checking the claims.

**Evidence:** [[human-approval]] and [[automated-decision-making]] (ICO draft guidance, UNVERIFIED, in [[02-ai-threats-and-controls]]). Workforce effects: [[novice-uplift-and-deskilling]] (UNVERIFIED).

**Trade-off:** The handler still checks every claim, so the time saved per case is limited. Our reasoning.

**What would change it:** Our reasoning: nothing in the research supports removing the person.
