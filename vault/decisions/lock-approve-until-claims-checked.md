# Lock Approve until claims are checked

**Decision:** Approve stays locked until every claim is confirmed, edited or removed.

**Who decided:** Not tagged in the design document. It is rule 1 of the agent's own proposed rules in [[06-interface-design]].

**Why:** Citations alone do not prevent rubber-stamping.

**Evidence:** [[cognitive-forcing]] (abstract only, UNVERIFIED; the study did not test per-claim confirmation, so this applies a principle). [[automation-bias]] (UNVERIFIED). [[human-approval]]. [[governance-built-in]].

**Trade-off:** Slower. People rated such designs least favourably. Confirming may become a reflex: see [[routine-confirmation-dialogs]]. Stated as a limitation.

**What would change it:** Measuring it with real handlers is the first thing a pilot would do ([[07-ux-evidence]]).
