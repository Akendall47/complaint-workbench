# Cite public sources for the demo data

**Decision:** Settings says plainly that every complaint, customer and member of staff is synthetic, then lists what the demo data follows and the public source for each, with its date and link. Anything with no public source is listed as our assumption.

**Who decided:** Owner, 11 Nov: "note in settings where the mock data came from and ensure it is a credible source so when Ankura assess this dashboard it makes sense". Which sources: a researcher agent checked each at source.

**Sources used (verified):** FCA "How to complain" (8 weeks; 15 and 35 business days for payment services); FCA complaints data 2026 H1 (1.85 million complaints, 44.8% closed within 3 days, 54.8% upheld); FCA aggregate complaints data 2025 H2 (banking and credit cards 857,757 of 1,740,909); Financial Ombudsman Service annual data 2025/26 (current accounts 32,900, of which fraud and scams 18,900); UK Finance Fraud Report 2026 (APP fraud £576.4m, 248,070 cases); FCA FG21/1 (vulnerability definition); Payment Systems Regulator APP reimbursement (from 7 October 2024, £85,000 cap).

**Assumptions (no public source found):** complaint channel mix, time of day, the volume ramp, the 34% return rate, the handover field list.

**Not used:** DISP rule text (the Handbook pages could not be opened by the tool, so no DISP quotes appear on the page).

**Shows in:** Settings, "Where the data comes from". Related: [[synthetic-data-only]], [[show-where-data-comes-from]], [[verify-before-quoting]]
