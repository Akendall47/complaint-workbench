# Three roles with a switcher

**Decision:** Three roles (handler, specialist, team lead) chosen from a switcher in the header; no real login.

**Who decided:** My call.

**Why:** Real authentication is a non-goal. The switcher lets a reviewer see each role's view. The role is recorded in the audit log with each action.

**Evidence:** No research source: our reasoning. Role effects are discussed in [[novice-uplift-and-deskilling]] (UNVERIFIED, [[05-ankura-solution-sets]]). See also [[stated-non-goals]].

**Trade-off:** No real authentication or access control. Stated as a limitation.

**What would change it:** Our reasoning: any use beyond a prototype.
