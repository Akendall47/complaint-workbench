# Five case states

**Decision:** A case is New, In review, Handed over, Returned or Accepted; a second return goes to the team lead.

**Who decided:** My call, including the second-return rule.

**Why:** Every case is handed to a department, including General service. Accepted is the end state. Resolving and closing the complaint is outside the prototype. A returned case goes back to the top of its handler's queue.

**Evidence:** No research source: our reasoning. It answers the brief's incomplete handovers through the [[returned-case-loop]] and the [[handover-pack]] (field list is an assumption, [[01-complaint-rules]]).

**Trade-off:** Our reasoning: the flow stops before the complaint is resolved, so deadline handling after handover is not shown.

**What would change it:** The owner can overrule a my-call item.
