Rules in use

Routing threshold
A complaint goes to a department only when the model is at least {{ cfg.routing_threshold }} sure; otherwise it shows as Needs routing.
Deadlines
Payment complaints: {{ cfg.deadlines.payment.business_days }} business days, or {{ cfg.deadlines.payment.extended_business_days }} when extended. Other complaints: {{ cfg.deadlines.other.weeks }} weeks.
Unusual rise
A department and type is flagged when this week has at least {{ cfg.rise.ratio }} times its usual weekly cases (average of the last {{ cfg.rise.baseline_weeks }} weeks) and at least {{ cfg.rise.minimum_cases }} cases.
Urgent
A case is urgent when its deadline is past or within {{ cfg.urgent.deadline_within_business_days }} business days, or its notes mention a vulnerable customer, money hardship or ongoing fraud. A person can also set it by hand, with a reason.

Database notes
  • The tool reads the bank's systems and writes back only the handover it sends.
  • Personal details are masked before any text reaches the model.
  • The audit log accepts new rows only, enforced by a database trigger in the prototype.
  • SQLite suits one demo user. A real deployment would use the bank's own database with access by role.
  • Overview figures show their refresh time so nobody acts on stale counts.
